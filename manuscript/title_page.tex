\noindent\textbf{Biographical note}

\noindent Boggavarapu Kiran is a computational scientist in the Department of Chemistry and Physics at McNeese State University. His methodological work concerns simulation as a tool for deriving the consequences of specified structural assumptions, an approach he brings from computational physical science to the study of institutions. His current research applies agent-based modelling to enforcement dynamics in codified communities, with particular attention to the structural conditions under which sanctioning authority concentrates and membership becomes durable. He works on the historical and comparative application of this framework to religious and colonial institutional regimes.
